# Key researchers

---

## Ultraviolet phototherapy in skin-limited malignancy

- **EORTC cutaneous lymphoma consensus groups**
  - Relevance: clinical protocols and evidence syntheses for ultraviolet-based phototherapy in cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (mycosis fungoides).
  - Representative reading: [EORTC consensus recommendations (update 2023; full text)](https://www.ejcancer.com/article/S0959-8049(23)00645-7/fulltext)

- **National Cancer Institute (NCI) PDQ editorial boards**
  - Relevance: treatment summaries for cutaneous T-cell lymphoma including phototherapy options and clinical framing.
  - Representative reading: [NCI PDQ: Mycosis Fungoides and the Sézary Syndrome Treatment](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK65849/)

- **Robert S. Stern**
  - Relevance: long-term outcome constraints for psoralen + ultraviolet A (PUVA) regimens via cumulative-dose carcinogenicity in skin.
  - Representative reading: [Stern et al., Risk of cutaneous squamous-cell carcinoma in patients treated with PUVA (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJM198405033101805)

## Melanin-targeted radionuclide therapy (melanotic melanoma)

- **Uwe Haberkorn and collaborators**
  - Relevance: early clinical experience with melanin-binding benzamide targeted radionuclide therapy for metastatic melanoma; dosimetry and selection by uptake.
  - Representative reading: [Radiopharmaceutical Therapy of Patients with Metastasized Melanoma with the Melanin-Binding Benzamide 131I-BA52 (J Nucl Med)](https://jnm.snmjournals.org/content/55/1/9)

---

## Vitamin D supplementation trials and meta-analyses

- **JoAnn E. Manson and colleagues**
  - Relevance: large randomized controlled trial evidence for vitamin D supplementation and cancer outcomes (VITAL).
  - Representative reading: [Manson et al., Vitamin D Supplements and Prevention of Cancer and Cardiovascular Disease (NEJM, 2019; VITAL primary)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1809944)

- **Naomi Keum and colleagues**
  - Relevance: randomized-trial meta-analysis separating total cancer incidence from total cancer mortality.
  - Representative reading: [Keum et al., Vitamin D supplementation and total cancer incidence and mortality: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (Annals of Oncology, 2019; full text)](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(19)31159-7/fulltext)

---

## Melanin pathway pharmacology (speculative adjunct; biomarker endpoints)

- **Ross StC Barnetson, Gary M. Halliday, and colleagues**
  - Relevance: randomized trial showing that a synthetic melanotropin increases pigmentation and reduces ultraviolet injury biomarkers (sunburn cells; thymine dimers) in fair-skinned volunteers; this is not evidence of cancer treatment and is only relevant here as a mechanistic lever for the speculative MC1R branch in `06_interventions.md`/`07_protocol.md`.
  - Representative reading: [Barnetson et al., Nle4-D-Phe7 alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone significantly increased pigmentation and decreased UV damage in fair-skinned Caucasian volunteers (J Invest Dermatol, 2006; PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16763547/)
