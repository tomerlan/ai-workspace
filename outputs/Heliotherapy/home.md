# Photomelanin

Melanin is the primary interface between light and human biology — a broadband photon-absorbing polymer distributed across skin, retina, cochlea, cardiac tissue, and brain. Its adaptive state determines mutagenic output of UV exposure and the electrical state of cells that contain it. The melanin-UV axis is one of the most actionable levers in cancer biology. It is essentially untouched by oncology.

Two interventions are developed here. The first rebuilds the skin's photoprotective and DNA-repair-competent state through graduated UV exposure. The second uses the melanin-melanosome system as a candidate transducer to shift the bioelectric phenotype of tumor cells. Neither requires a novel drug. Both require treating light as a primary biological variable.

---

## Who This Is For

**Cancer patients** — any patient in the typical oncology state: chronically indoors, vitamin D depleted, light-naive. Standard care does not address the photobiological dimension. These protocols do.

**Prevention** — building and maintaining an adaptive photoprotective state before pathology develops.

**Clinicians and researchers** — seeking a mechanistic framework for light as a therapeutic variable and a defined preclinical program to test it.

## The Core Argument

- The melanin-UV axis is one of the most actionable levers in cancer biology. It is essentially untouched by oncology.
- Melanin is not a pigment — it is a mixed ionic-electronic conductor distributed across skin, cochlea, cardiac tissue, leptomeninges, and RPE, whose photophysical state couples to cell electrophysiology.
- The operative variable is the **eu:pheo ratio**: eumelanin dissipates UV energy; pheomelanin generates ROS under UVA and drives DNA damage independently of UV, including in darkness.
- UV exposure is a vector — spectrum × intermittency × geometry × host modifiers. Burns and chronic adaptive exposure are mechanistically different. The evidence separates them; public health policy does not.
- Vitamin D supplementation trials (VITAL, N=25,871) found no cancer incidence reduction. This closes the supplementation question. Sunlight drives co-pathways — NO mobilization, immune calibration, melanin electrophysiology — that no pill replicates.
- Heliotherapy worked. Finsen won the Nobel Prize for it in 1903. Rollier treated 2,000+ patients across 36 Alpine clinics. The practice was displaced by antibiotics, not by failure.

## Intervention 1 — Solar Callus Protocol

Graduated suberythemal UV exposure that co-induces:
- Eumelanin synthesis and supranuclear cap geometry in keratinocytes
- Epidermal hyperplasia (MED increases 1.4–2.3× in trials)
- NER upregulation via MC1R/cAMP/CREB
- Post-UV oxidative conditioning (chemiexcitation window management)
- Photoimmune calibration

**For cancer patients:** restores the photobiological baseline that standard oncological guidance systematically dismantles. Also the substrate prerequisite for Intervention 2.
**For prevention:** builds the adaptive state that separates low-risk chronic outdoor exposures from high-risk intermittent burns.
**Benchmark:** MED ≥2× baseline. **Quality marker:** eu:pheo ratio by HPLC-AHPO.

## Intervention 2 — Light-Driven Electrophysiology

- Cancer cells are depolarized (Vmem ≈ −10 to −30 mV vs. −50 to −90 mV in normal cells) with alkaline intracellular pH — both causally upstream of proliferation.
- Proposed mechanism: photon absorption in the melanosome → proton release from polymer surface → modulation of melanosomal ion transporters → cytoplasmic pHi shift → membrane hyperpolarization. Targets the bioelectric state of the tumor, not a specific oncogene.
- Wavelength by depth: UVA (surface) → violet/blue (shallow) → red 630–680 nm (deeper lesions).
- Eligibility gate: melanin confirmed in target tissue by T1-MRI, dermoscopy, or biopsy.
- **Status:** mechanism grounded in published biophysics; chain as a unit not yet demonstrated in cells. Seven-experiment preclinical program defined.

## Key Researchers

**Michael Holick, MD PhD** (Boston University) — leading authority on sunlight dosing and vitamin D metabolism; skin-type and latitude-specific UV protocols; directly opposes the sun-avoidance consensus.

**Cedric Garland, DrPH** (UC San Diego) — DINOMIT model of vitamin D and cancer prevention; primary author of the systematic dose-response epidemiological literature on vitamin D and internal cancers.

**Auguste Rollier** (1874–1954, Leysin) — the definitive heliotherapy practitioner; 36 clinics, explicitly graduated sunlight protocols, largest published clinical record of therapeutic sun exposure.

**Jack Kruse, MD** (Kruse Longevity Center) — neurosurgeon turned quantum biology theorist; most vocal clinical voice on native light environment as the primary cancer variable; frames melanin as a photoelectric transducer, mitochondrial dysfunction as the cancer origin, and UV/IR at sunrise as the corrective input. Divisive figure; core directional claim that light is the reference biological signal is consistent with the framework here.

**Riordan Clinic** (Wichita, KS) — non-profit integrative oncology, operating since 1975; targets 25(OH)D at 60–100 ng/mL; most credible active center working on the sunlight-cancer axis.
