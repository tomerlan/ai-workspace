## Cardiac Disease / Heart Failure

**Claims:**
1. Melanin degradation in the cardiac plexus leads to supraventricular tachycardia (SVT).
2. Blue light and non-native electromagnetic fields (nnEMF) liberate Vitamin A, leading to melanin degradation into adrenaline and noradrenaline, stimulating the heart adrenergically.
3. Intense red light can mimic adenosine's effects, potentially protecting cardiovascular tissues by targeting the PER2 gene.

**Mechanism:**
- Melanin degradation results in increased adrenergic stimulation.
- Red light influences circadian genes (PER2), enhancing cardiac resilience under low oxygen conditions.

**Assessment:**
- **Speculative:** The link between melanin degradation and SVT lacks direct evidence.
- **Partially Supported:** The role of red light in circadian rhythm regulation is supported, but its equivalence to adenosine in cardiac protection is speculative.

**Bottom Line:**
The defensible kernel is the influence of light on circadian rhythms and potential cardiac effects. The overreach is the direct causation of heart conditions through melanin degradation without sufficient evidence.
