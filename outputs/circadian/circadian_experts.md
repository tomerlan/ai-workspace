## Chronotherapy and Cancer

### Francis Lévi

**Affiliation:** Paris-Saclay University (emeritus); Paul Brousse Hospital; formerly Warwick University

**Work:** Pioneer of cancer chronotherapy. Demonstrated that drug efficacy and toxicity vary by time of day; developed programmable circadian drug-delivery pumps. Found circadian time-dependency in 20+ anticancer agents; identified rhythms in detoxification, proliferation, metabolism, and DNA repair. Founded EORTC Chronotherapy Group; led landmark trials of chronomodulated oxaliplatin/5-FU in colorectal cancer. Co-authored LungTIME-C01 (immunochemotherapy timing in NSCLC).

**Link:** [Warwick Chronotherapy Group](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/med/research/biomedical/labs/rdallmann/dallmann/chronotherapy/about/) | [Oncopedia profile](https://www.oncopedia.wiki/key-players/francis-levi)

---

### Christoph Scheiermann

**Affiliation:** University of Geneva; Ludwig-Maximilians University Munich

**Work:** Circadian immunology. Studies how leukocyte recruitment and immune response oscillate over the day. Showed dendritic cell migration from skin to lymph nodes is circadian; tumors implanted during immune active phase grow more slowly. Demonstrated time-of-day dependence of immunotherapy. Co-authored LungTIME-C01. ERC and Emmy-Noether awards.

**Link:** [Scheiermann Lab](https://scheiermannlab.de/) | [Geneva Centre for Inflammation Research](https://www.unige.ch/medecine/gcir/about-us/christoph-scheiermann)

## Core Circadian Biology

### Russell Foster

**Affiliation:** University of Oxford; Nuffield Laboratory of Ophthalmology; Sleep and Circadian Neuroscience Institute (SCNi)

**Work:** Discovered photosensitive retinal ganglion cells (pRGCs) and their role in non-image-forming vision. Showed rods and cones are not required for circadian entrainment. Extensive work on melanopsin, melanopic sensitivity, and light input to the SCN. Honma Prize, Cogan Award.

**Link:** [Circadian and Visual Neuroscience Lab](https://www.ndcn.ox.ac.uk/research/circadian-and-visual-neuroscience-foster) | [SCNi](https://www.scni.ox.ac.uk/team/russell-foster)

---

### Steven Lockley

**Affiliation:** Harvard Medical School; Brigham and Women's Hospital; Division of Sleep and Circadian Disorders

**Work:** Human circadian physiology, light, and melatonin. Demonstrated high sensitivity of circadian melatonin rhythm to short-wavelength (blue) light; established spectral sensitivity for phase resetting and melatonin suppression. Shift work, light at night, and health. Circadian Physiology Program at BWH.

**Link:** [Harvard Division of Sleep Medicine](https://sleep.hms.harvard.edu/faculty-staff/steven-w-lockley) | [Circadian Physiology Program](https://circadian.bwh.harvard.edu/)

---

### Charles Czeisler

**Affiliation:** Harvard Medical School; Brigham and Women's Hospital; Division of Sleep Medicine

**Work:** SCN neurobiology, light and circadian resetting, melanopsin, sleep homeostasis. 40+ years on light–physiology relationship; effects of sleep restriction, aging, shift work. National Sleep Foundation Lifetime Achievement Award. Teaches Circadian Biology at Harvard.

## Cancer and Circadian

### David Blask

**Affiliation:** Tulane University School of Medicine; Laboratory of Chrono-Neuroendocrine Oncology

**Work:** 35+ years on melatonin and cancer. Melatonin as anticarcinogenic signal; inhibits tumor uptake of linoleic acid, suppresses Warburg metabolism, disrupts cAMP signaling. Light at night suppresses melatonin and stimulates tumor growth. Aaron B. Lerner Pioneer Medal for melatonin research.

**Link:** [Tulane profile](https://medicine.tulane.edu/departments/structural-cellular-biology-tulane-center-aging-center-circadian-biology-tulane-0)

---

### Satchidananda Panda

**Affiliation:** Salk Institute for Biological Studies; Rita and Richard Atkinson Chair

**Work:** Time-restricted eating (TRE), circadian metabolism. Found that confining eating to 8–12 h improves metabolic health without caloric restriction; ~70% of mouse genes respond to TRE. Human studies on diabetes, obesity, shift workers. Co-discovered melanopsin. Developed myCircadianClock app.

**Link:** [Salk Institute profile](https://www.salk.edu/scientist/satchidananda-panda/)

---

### Loning Fu

**Affiliation:** Baylor College of Medicine; Dan L Duncan Comprehensive Cancer Center

**Work:** Circadian clock genes and tumor suppression. Period2 in DNA damage response and tumor suppression; clock-deficient mice show increased spontaneous and radiation-induced tumors. Central clock regulates sympathetic signaling, p53, cell cycle. Clock dysfunction, obesity, and hepatocellular carcinoma.

**Link:** [Baylor College of Medicine](https://www.bcm.edu/people-search/loning-fu-21668)

## Measurement and Phase

### Alfred Lewy

**Affiliation:** Oregon Health and Science University

**Work:** Developed DLMO (dim-light melatonin onset) as gold-standard circadian phase marker. Melatonin treatment for blind individuals, shift workers, SAD, advanced/delayed sleep phase. Phase response curve for light and melatonin; clinical applications for circadian disorders.

**Link:** [OHSU provider profile](https://www.ohsu.edu/providers/alfred-j-lewy-md-phd)

---

### Helen Burgess

**Affiliation:** University of Michigan; Sleep and Circadian Research Laboratory

**Work:** Light and circadian phase; morning light for phase advance, evening light and melatonin suppression. Bright light therapy for depression, chronic pain. Screen use before bed and disease risk. Practical guidance on light timing for health.

**Link:** [Michigan Medicine profile](https://medicine.umich.edu/dept/prechter-program/helen-j-burgess-phd)

## Extended Frameworks

### Jack Kruse

**Affiliation:** Independent; neurosurgeon, health educator

**Work:** Circadian framework emphasizing light environment, spectral quality, mitochondria. Light as primary regulator; morning red/NIR priming, solar sequencing (morning → midday → night). Leptin–melanocortin as circadian–metabolic integrator; mitochondria as executors of circadian programs. Cancer as failure of temporal and redox control.

**Link:** [jackkruse.com](https://jackkruse.com/) | [Circadian Cycle articles](https://jackkruse.com/category/circadian-cycle/)

---

### Zaid K. Dahhaj

**Affiliation:** Independent; circadian biology educator, coach

**Work:** Day/night segregation; strict temporal discipline. Artificial light at night counteracts daytime sunlight; non-linear circadian responses. Morning priming (red/NIR, MSH), evening downshifting. UV as hormetic stressor; sunscreen/glasses may block needed signaling. Brighter days, darker nights; meal timing and movement as zeitgebers.

**Link:** [zaidkdahhaj.com](https://www.zaidkdahhaj.com/) | [Substack](https://zaidkdahhaj.substack.com/)
